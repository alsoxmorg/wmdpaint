Current limitations
===================
- Clipboard output uses wmdpaint's private format; standard PNG output is planned.
- 1fpirate cannot preserve colour or alpha. Enabling monochrome mode destroys
  existing colours; undoing its toggle does not recover them.
- Undo is bounded and in memory; large document snapshots can consume memory.
- Allocation-failure handling across multi-layer operations needs further work.
- The UI startup smoke test requires Xvfb and skips if it is unavailable.

Feature priorities and deferred scope are maintained in TODO.txt.
The historical SDL3 build errors previously listed here have been superseded
by the compatibility wrappers and current build checks.
